Export your Slack to-dos

Save message text, sender names, and links as TXT, CSV, or JSON. No installation or API key needed.

1. Add the bookmark

Press Ctrl + Shift + B to show the bookmarks bar. Drag this button onto it:

Export Slack to-dos

Run the bookmark from your browser bar while viewing Slack.

2. Open To-dos in Slack

Open Slack in this browser, choose your workspace, and open To-dos. Choose In-progress for unfinished tasks, or include all statuses and reminders for everything.

3. Collect and download

Click the bookmark, then Collect all shown to-dos. Keep the tab open while it scrolls. Check the final count against Slack, then choose a download format.

Export each workspace or view separately. Leave filters unchanged while collecting. Download before closing, refreshing, or changing views.

Capture stays local. The tool does not read tokens or cookies, post to Slack, or upload files. Only loaded items are available; custom tasks may lack a sender or link. Files are saved as links.

If the panel finds no items or the bookmark does nothing

Zero items? Choose Pick a to-do, click a task's text, then collect again. Escape cancels picking. If that fails, Download page structure provides a diagnostic without message text or links.

Cannot drag? Create a bookmark, edit its URL, and paste the full value below. Your browser or workplace may block bookmarklets.
